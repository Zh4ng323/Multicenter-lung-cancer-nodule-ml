# Lung Cancer Risk-Stratified Screening & Health Guidance

Based on the *Chinese Guidelines for Lung Cancer Screening and Early Diagnosis (2023 Edition)*.
For health guidance only; does not replace clinical diagnosis.

---

## 🟢 Low Risk — Baseline Prevention

**Screening**
- Low-dose CT (LDCT): every 3 years
- Annual pulmonary function test + tumor markers

**Lifestyle**
- Avoid all tobacco smoke and cooking fumes
- Regular aerobic exercise; adequate sleep
- Light diet with lung-nourishing fruits and vegetables

**When to See a Doctor**
- Only if persistent cough exceeds 2 weeks → respiratory clinic follow-up

---

## 🟡 Medium Risk — Annual Monitoring

**Screening**
- LDCT: annually (same institution for image comparison)
- Annual pulmonary function test + full tumor marker panel
- Follow small nodules per physician guidance; avoid unnecessary biopsy

**Lifestyle**
- Quit smoking immediately; minimize air pollution exposure
- Wear N95 mask on smoggy/dusty days
- Breathing exercises; limit spicy food and alcohol

**When to See a Doctor**
- Cough > 3 weeks, blood-tinged sputum, or unexplained fatigue/weight loss → schedule LDCT before next annual check

---

## 🔴 High Risk — Intensive Follow-up

**Screening**
- Thin-slice LDCT: every 6 months
- Semi-annual tumor markers + inflammatory markers
- Establish a dedicated imaging archive with a thoracic surgery / respiratory specialist

**Lifestyle**
- Permanent smoking cessation (including e-cigarettes)
- Gentle exercise only; avoid strenuous activity
- Treat underlying COPD / old tuberculosis per standard protocols
- Annual influenza + pneumococcal vaccination recommended

**Red-Flag Symptoms — Seek Immediate Care**
- Hemoptysis, persistent chest pain, dyspnea, rapid unexplained weight loss, recurrent low-grade fever → emergency thoracic surgery consultation

---

*Disclaimer: This stratified guidance is for screening reference only and does not constitute clinical diagnosis or treatment. All findings require specialist evaluation combining imaging, history, and clinical judgment.*
