The datasets in this directory (`internal_test_subset.csv`,
`external_subset.csv`) are fully synthetic. Every row is machine-generated from
per-class statistical summaries (means and shrunk covariances) of the real
cohorts; no real participant data are included.

You are free to share and adapt the material with attribution to the paper,
including for commercial purposes.
